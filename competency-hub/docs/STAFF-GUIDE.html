ESG Competency Hub – how to

The Hub keeps our actions, notices, improvements and degradations, and monthly meetings in one place. Open it from the link in the email and bookmark it.

Your week

The home page shows this week (your deadlines and meetings), your actions (late, due this week, coming up), and the ten areas. Click an area to open it in place; ✕ or Esc closes it. Every area is visible to everyone; you can add to the areas you belong to.

Actions

  • Open an action by clicking it. You can Add an update, say I need more time (with a reason), or Mark as done. More options has the rest, if you're allowed.
  • Raise an action with the button at the top: what needs doing, whose it is and by when.
  • Most changes show being recorded for a minute or two, then Saved. You can carry on meanwhile. Updates are saved at once.
  • You'll get an email when an action is given to you, a reminder 7 days before it's due, and reminders if it's late.

Notices and issues

  • Post a notice in any area. Open a notice to read it in full and comment.
  • Flag for the next meeting puts it on the area's next agenda. It clears once the meeting has discussed it.
  • Raise an issue records an improvement or a degradation; it can have actions linked to it.

Meetings

  • Each area's monthly meeting is in its panel and in your week. Open it to see the agenda; Add to my calendar if the invitation didn't reach you.
  • If you run meetings: Book a meeting sends the Outlook invitation. On the day, Start meeting fixes the agenda; then go through it one item at a time, record attendance, outcomes, notes and decisions, and raise or update actions from an item. Review and finalise checks that nothing is missing, then makes the minutes final, with a PDF.
  • Finalised minutes can be read, printed or downloaded by everyone.

Feedback

The Feedback button at the bottom of every page: report a problem, ask a question or suggest an improvement. It goes straight to the Hub's administrators.

Text too small? Use Text size at the top right. Everything works with the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to open, Esc to close.
