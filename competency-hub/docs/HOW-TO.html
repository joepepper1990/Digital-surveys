Step 1 – app 1.4.1

  1. Open Site contents, then Welcome Web Part - Custom Layouts. Upload both files from 3-App and choose Replace for each.
  2. Open the Hub. Edit the web part, click Reset layout cache, republish, then press Ctrl+F5. The footer should say version 1.4.1, and the line under the top bar Everything is up to date.

Step 2 – solution 1.10.1.0

  1. Open Power Automate and go to Solutions → Import solution. Choose ESGCompetencyHub_1_10_1_0_unmanaged.zip and import it as an upgrade, with Enable plugin steps and flows unticked, as before.
  2. The upgrade leaves the replaced flows Off ("deactivated and replaced"). In Solutions → ESG Competency Hub, turn On, one at a time:
    • 1 Daily tasks, 2 Request queue, 3 Queue monitor, 4 Records;
    • 5 Meetings, 6 Administration, 7 Minutes.
    Leave 8 Clean-up and the two probes Off.
  3. Nothing needs setting: 2 Request queue, 4 Records and 5 Meetings work on up to five changes at once; 6 Administration and 8 Clean-up now work on one at a time.
  4. If a flow refuses to turn on, send me its error.

Step 3 – try it (as yourself, about 10 minutes)

Use TEST in titles. Every change shows at once with a small ring that fills; a green tick circle shows and fades when it is saved, usually within a minute.

  1. One change. Mark a TEST to-do card as done. The line under the top bar says Waiting to be picked up, then Being recorded, then the green tick shows. Click Reopen: it comes back the same way.
  2. Five quick changes on one action. Open a TEST action and, without waiting: add an update, move the deadline, change its status, edit its title, mark it done. All five show at once and are saved one after another, in that order, in about 4–5 minutes in all. None says "changed by someone else".
  3. Five quick changes on five actions. Mark five different TEST cards as done as fast as you can. All five are saved within about 1–2 minutes.
  4. A meeting, quickly. Book a TEST Flask meeting, open it at once and click Start meeting. Tick attendance, record an outcome, type a note and click the next item: the note stays. On an action item, click Close this action and keep typing while it saves: nothing you typed moves or goes, and the window stays open. Then Finalise: the minutes show at once, and Open the PDF appears within a few minutes.
  5. Administration, first tab: How long changes take lists what you just did. Send me a screenshot.

What changes

For people using the Hub

  • Screens are quicker after a change is saved. The page reads only the record that changed, not the whole list again, and several changes saved together are read in one go.
  • A busy SharePoint never empties a list. If a list can't be read, what was last read stays on screen, however many times it fails, and it is tried again a minute later, then less often while it keeps failing.
  • A change that goes wrong is held, not sent blindly. If an earlier change it depends on was refused (for example a booking that failed, with a Start waiting behind it), the ones behind it wait with one card offering Try again or Discard.
  • Windows: one you have typed in still closes only with its × or Cancel. Once what you typed has been sent, it counts as untouched again, so Escape closes it as usual; choosing attendance never counts as typing.
  • Timing words match what really happens: "in about a minute" instead of "1–3 minutes", and the ring's own estimate.
  • The ring's estimates learn only from normal changes: one that needed an administrator, finished while the tab was hidden or took far longer than usual is left out.

Behind the scenes

  • Cancelling a meeting no longer locks its record if the cancel is refused; amending minutes no longer leaves an amendment behind when the amendment is refused.
  • The queue monitor never overwrites a change that has just finished, and no longer reports a flow as down because of a short backlog: a change counts as stuck after 15 minutes (30 for minutes and clean-up), and as not picked up after 20.
  • A change that finds its record being updated waits for it only when waiting can help; otherwise it is refused at once, with a plain message.
  • Linking an action to another area waits until any change to that action has finished.
  • Two administrators at once can no longer remove each other's rights: 6 Administration works on one change at a time.
  • Fewer SharePoint calls: raising an action takes 20 calls (was 23), moving a deadline or marking done 17 (was 18); a quiet queue-monitor run takes 7, and one outside its hours 1. That is about 5,000 fewer Power Automate actions a week.
  • Queue monitor hours (Administration → Settings): the minutes between checks outside working hours must be 20 to 90, in tens, because the monitor runs every 10 minutes.
