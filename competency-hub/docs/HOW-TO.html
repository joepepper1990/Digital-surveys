ESG Competency Hub – Releases 1 to 6

This package brings the Hub from app 0.3.0 and solution 1.2.0.0 (what you have now) to app 1.0.0, solution 1.8.0.2 and Setup 1.3.2. Each release includes the ones before it, so you can install this package directly. Updated 23 October 2026.

  • Release 1, safety net: a queue monitor that recovers stalled changes and retries emails; clear messages while a change is waiting, being recorded, being retried or needs an administrator; an Administration page; and a reduced mode in which the buttons that send changes are paused while changes can't be processed.
  • Release 2, administration and permissions: add people, choose their areas, meeting rights, access end dates and notice posting from the app; the corrected technician and meeting rights; Feedback list and Meeting Minutes library ready for later releases.
  • Release 3, noticeboard, register, feedback, settings and dashboard: read a notice in full, comment, flag it for the next meeting; edit, pin and archive notices; improvements and degradations with linked actions; a Feedback button on every page with a review tab for administrators; a Settings tab; and a Management page for Team Leaders and the Group Head.
  • Release 4, meetings I: book monthly meetings, with an Outlook invitation from your calendar, an update when the meeting changes and a cancellation when it is cancelled; Add to my calendar for everyone; the agenda, previewed before the meeting and fixed when it starts; items added by hand; and a probe flow to prove the invitations first.
  • Release 5, meetings II: run the meeting in the Hub – attendance, each item's outcome and notes, decisions, raising or changing actions from an item; finalise, which makes the record read-only; minutes with a PDF to open, download or print; amendments by Team Leaders and the Group Head; flags cleared once an item is discussed; and a probe flow to prove the PDF conversion first.
  • Release 6, checks and go-live: Administration → Remove test data, with a flow of its own; what you post directly is marked as a test record until then; and the checks before go-live (security, list size, accessibility). The steps for the day itself are in GO-LIVE.html, and the one-page guide for staff in STAFF-GUIDE.html.

Everything here was built and tested on a simulated SharePoint site only. Each part below changes your Competency Hub site or your Power Automate environment, so do it only when you are ready. Nobody else gets access until you add them.

Part A – Setup 1.3.2 (about 15 minutes)

Setup only adds things; it removes no data. It adds the Feedback list, the Meeting Minutes library, a few new columns and settings, the group Competency Hub – Notice Posters, the meetings' end time, and two switches under Settings (Outlook invitations and PDF minutes, both on). It makes one permission change: the Noticeboard goes from "All Users can add" to "All Users can read, Notice Posters can add", so an administrator can stop one person posting. You are the only user, so nobody else is affected.

  1. Upload both files from 1-Setup to Welcome Web Part - Custom Layouts (Replace).
  2. Create the HubSetup page again with a Welcome web part, choose the layout Competency Hub Setup, and publish it.
  3. Click Check. It writes nothing. Send me the log.
  4. When I have looked at it, click Apply, type SETUP and wait for it to finish. Send me the log.
  5. Delete the two Setup files and the HubSetup page again.

Part B – solution 1.8.0.2 (about 10 minutes)

  1. In Power Automate, same environment: Solutions → Import solution → Browse, choose ESGCompetencyHub_1_8_0_2_unmanaged.zip, Next. It should say upgrade. Leave Enable Plugin steps and flows included within the solution unticked: you turn on the right flows yourself below.
  2. Keep the SharePoint and Outlook connections and the site address. For the new OneDrive for Business connection, choose (or create) one with your own account. Then Import. If the import says the OneDrive connector is not allowed with the others, that is the data policy: send me the message, and in the meantime set PDF minutes to No (Part H).
  3. Open the solution. There are now ten flows. Make sure these five are On:
    • ESG Competency Hub | 1 Daily tasks and 2 Request queue (check they are still on – an upgrade can switch them off);
    • ESG Competency Hub | 3 Queue monitor, 4 Records and 6 Administration (new: turn them on).
    Leave 5 Meetings, 7 Minutes, 8 Clean-up and the two probes Off for now: Parts G, H and the go-live note turn them on when they are needed.

Part D – try the safety net (Release 1)

  1. On the home page, click Open Administration. The first card should say Automatic processing is working once the queue monitor has run (within 15 minutes in working hours).
  2. Turn 2 Request queue Off. Raise a TEST action. It shows waiting to be recorded.
  3. Within about 25 minutes the monitor notices that nothing is being picked up. The app then shows a banner saying changes can't be processed, and pauses the buttons that send changes. That is expected: it is what staff would see.
  4. Turn 2 Request queue back On. Within 15 minutes the monitor sends the request again, the action appears without you doing anything else, and the banner goes at the next check.
  5. Send me a screenshot of the Administration page and the run history of 3 Queue monitor.

The same banner appears if the monitor itself stops. Reading and adding updates always keep working. If only 6 Administration is off, staff carry on as normal and the Administration page says so.

Part E – administration and the permission probe (Release 2)

This needs one ordinary account that is not a site owner, for example a test account from IT or a colleague for one session. A site owner can't be used: owners bypass permissions, so every check would pass without proving anything.

Add the test account

  1. Administration → People → Add people. Enter the test account's email, role Technician.
  2. Tick Flask as a member (leave Can run meetings off), and tick Green Stream with Can run meetings on. Leave Can post notices on. Click Add this person; it shows Done and verified after a minute or two.
  3. Open your own name in the list: it should say you can't change your own access.

Run the probe as the test account

  1. As yourself: upload both files from 4-Permission-probe to Welcome Web Part - Custom Layouts, create a page HubProbe2 with a Welcome web part, choose the layout Competency Hub probe P2, and publish it.
  2. Sign in as the test account (a private browser window is easiest) and open the HubProbe2 page.
  3. Click Run read checks, then Run write checks. The write checks wait for the flow, so allow up to 10 minutes. They add one TEST notice and up to two TEST requests, all marked as test records. The second request raises a TEST action for a Team Leader, so that person (you, if you're the first one listed) gets an "assigned to you" email.
  4. Copy everything in the results box and send it to me. The box hides names, email addresses and the site address.
  5. Then, as yourself: in People, open the test account and turn Can post notices off, Save changes. Run the write checks again as the test account and send the results; the notice check should now show it is refused.

Tidy up

  1. Delete the probe files and the HubProbe2 page.
  2. Keep the test account until we've read the results. Then, in People, open it and choose Mark as left.

Part F – notices, the register, feedback, settings and the dashboard (Release 3)

All of this can be tried as yourself. Use TEST in titles so the clean-up in Release 6 can find them.

Notices and comments

  1. On the home page, open Flask. The middle column, Notices & issues, now lists notices and open improvements and degradations. Click a notice: it opens in full.
  2. Add a comment. It appears at once.
  3. Click Flag for the next meeting, then Withdraw the flag. Both are immediate; Discussion history lists them.
  4. Click Pin, then Edit and change the title. Each shows being recorded and then the change, usually within two minutes.
  5. On a comment, click Hide. It disappears once recorded.

Improvements and degradations

  1. In the Flask panel, click Raise an issue. Choose Degradation, give a TEST title and description, choose who is responsible, and send it. It appears in the list within two minutes as REG-0000n.
  2. Open it. Click Raise a linked action and raise a TEST action: it is listed under Linked actions. Or click Link an existing action.
  3. Click Change status, choose Resolved: it asks for an outcome. Enter one and save.
  4. The full area page has the same lists under Notices & issues, with a filter for the register.

Feedback, settings and the dashboard

  1. Click Feedback at the bottom right of any page, send a TEST question, and wait for Thanks – your feedback has been sent.
  2. Administration → Feedback: your item is listed. Open it, write a response, set it to Answered.
  3. Administration → Settings: change Due soon from 7 to 10 days and back. Each change is recorded within a few minutes; try 40 to see it refused.
  4. Management (in the top bar): click any number. The list underneath is exactly the actions that number counts.
  5. Send me screenshots of the notice, the entry and the Management page.

If 4 Records is off, actions carry on as normal; changes to notices and the register are paused and the Administration page says so. Comments and flags still work, because they don't go through a flow.

Part G – meetings, invitations and the agenda (Release 4)

Invitations come from your calendar, with you as the organiser, so replies and acceptances come to you. You need the test colleague from Part E (or any colleague willing to receive three TEST emails).

First: the invitations probe

  1. In the solution, turn ESG Competency Hub | P-INV Invitations probe On, open it and click Run. Enter the colleague's email address and run it.
  2. It makes a TEST event for tomorrow at 12:00 with the two of you, moves it to 13:00, finds it again by its hidden reference, and cancels it. Your colleague should get the invitation, the update and the cancellation, a few seconds apart.
  3. Open the run and the last step, Probe results. Send me what it shows (it has no addresses in it), and ask your colleague what arrived.
  4. Turn the probe Off again.

If the probe fails, go to Administration → Settings and set Outlook invitations to No. Meetings are still booked and shown; everyone adds them to their calendar with Add to my calendar. Nothing needs rebuilding.

Then: book, change, start and cancel

  1. Turn ESG Competency Hub | 5 Meetings On.
  2. On the home page, open Flask and click Book a meeting. Choose a date this week, a TEST place, yourself as chair, and untick everyone except you and the test colleague (the attendees are Flask's members, so add your colleague to Flask first in People if they aren't). Click Book meeting. Within about two minutes it appears in the Flask panel and the week strip, and the invitation arrives.
  3. Click the meeting. It shows The Outlook invitation has gone to the attendees and an agenda preview. Under the agenda, add a TEST item: it appears under New business at once.
  4. Click Change and move it an hour. The update arrives in Outlook.
  5. Click Start meeting. Within about two minutes the meeting shows In progress and the agenda is fixed: it no longer says preview.
  6. Book a second TEST meeting and click Cancel meeting, giving a reason. The cancellation arrives.
  7. On the first meeting, click Add to my calendar: a calendar file downloads and opens in Outlook.
  8. Send me screenshots of the meeting before and after starting it.

If 5 Meetings is off, booking pauses and the Administration page says so; everything else carries on.

Part H – run a meeting, finalise it, and the PDF minutes (Release 5)

First: the PDF probe

  1. In the solution, turn ESG Competency Hub | P-PDF PDF probe On, open it and click Run. It asks for nothing.
  2. It writes a sample page to your OneDrive, turns it into a PDF, saves it in the Meeting Minutes library as TEST-PDF-probe-…pdf, and removes the working file.
  3. Open the run's last step, Probe results, and send me what it shows. Open the TEST PDF in Meeting Minutes and check it looks right, then delete it.
  4. Turn the probe Off again.

If the probe fails (for example the data policy blocks OneDrive), go to Administration → Settings and set PDF minutes to No. Finalised minutes then have a Print button, and the browser's Save as PDF makes the PDF. Nothing needs rebuilding.

Then: a whole TEST meeting

  1. Turn ESG Competency Hub | 7 Minutes On.
  2. Open the TEST meeting you started in Part G (or book and start a new one). As someone who runs Flask's meetings you now see the meeting one item at a time: choose an outcome, add notes, Save and next. Items for discussion are marked Needs an outcome.
  3. Under Attendance, mark yourself Present and your colleague Apologies. Under Decisions, record a TEST decision.
  4. On one item, click Raise an action and raise a TEST action. On an action item, click Open the action and close a TEST action. Both show being recorded.
  5. Click Review and finalise. It lists anything still needing an outcome and any change still being recorded; once there is nothing, Finalise the minutes.
  6. Within about two minutes the meeting shows Minutes finalised, with the minutes. Within a few more, Open the PDF and Download PDF appear. Check the PDF, and try Print.
  7. As yourself (a Team Leader), click Amend the minutes, say what and why, and send it. The amendment appears with the minutes, and a second PDF (-v2) appears beside the first.
  8. Open a notice that was discussed: its Discussion history lists the meeting and the outcome, and its flag is gone.
  9. Send me screenshots of the live meeting, the review, and the minutes.

The PDF is made after the minutes are finalised, separately: if it fails, the minutes stand and can be read and printed, the queue monitor tries again, and after three failures the administrators get an email. If 7 Minutes is off, finalising and amending pause; everything else carries on.

Part I – before go-live (Release 6)

  1. Post a TEST notice: until go-live it is marked as a test record, like everything the flows make.
  2. Open Administration → Remove test data and look: it lists every record marked as a test, and underneath, the records made before marking existed, to tick. Don't confirm yet – that is a go-live step.
  3. Follow GO-LIVE.html when you are ready to go live.
