ESG Competency Hub – installing release 1.2

25 October 2026. Release 1.2 is the version you approved in the preview. Install it in the order below: Setup first, then the solution, then the app. Allow about 30 minutes, plus a short session with Rich.

App 1.4.0 and solution 1.10.0.0 (29 October): quicker, and safe however fast people press. If you have already installed release 1.2, do Step 3 (the app files) first, then Step 2 (the solution), then the quick tests in Step 4b. The app works with the solution you have now; the solution is what makes saving quicker.
  • App 1.4.0 – windows close only when you close them. The meeting window could still close or empty itself when SharePoint was busy: a background re-read failed, and the app took the meeting as gone. Now what was last read is kept and read again a moment later, and the line under the top bar says SharePoint is busy – showing what was last read. A window you have typed or chosen something in closes only with its × or Cancel: not by a click outside, a drag that ends outside, or Escape. If anything odd happens, Feedback offers to include what the Hub noticed on your computer, so I can see exactly what went wrong.
  • App 1.3.2 – nothing you are typing is ever thrown away. The meeting screen (and the action and notice windows) used to rebuild themselves when a change finished saving, which wiped what you were typing; the live meeting screen also switched itself off while a change to the booking was saving, and the window shrank, so a click at its old edge closed it. Now what you have typed or chosen is kept through every refresh, with the cursor where it was; the live screen stays on whatever is saving; and an action opened from the agenda and closed again leaves the meeting still updating underneath.
  • App 1.3.1 – a progress ring instead of ✓ Sent. On every card, the line under the top bar and the open record, a small ring fills as the change goes through, with the stage and about how long is left: Waiting to be picked up · about 40 s, Being recorded · about 20 s, Any moment now. The times are learned from how long your own recent changes took (it says usually until it has a few to go on). It cannot be exact to the second: the flows don't report their steps, so the page sees three stages (sent, picked up, saved) and estimates the rest. The ring never fills until the change is really saved; then a green circle with a tick shows and fades.
  • App 1.3.0 – one press, one change. A button whose change is on its way is greyed until it returns, so a double click never sends twice. Changes to one record go out one at a time, in order, each against the revision the one before it left; changes to different records go out at once.
  • Nothing lost on reload. Changes waiting their turn, and anything recorded on a meeting booked a moment ago, are kept in the browser and sent after a reload. The browser warns before you leave while something is still only on your screen.
  • Notes are never thrown away: typed notes go with you when you click another agenda item, Next or Previous.
  • Everyone sees everyone. Other people's changes appear on your screen within a minute, and at once when you come back to the tab, without a reload; your own pending changes stay on top. A record someone else changed since you opened it says so under its title.
  • A real clash is explained. If someone else changed the same record first, the card names them and what they changed, and offers Apply mine on top. If your change can't clash with theirs (a different field), it is sent again by itself. If your own earlier change was refused, the ones behind it are held, not sent, with one card: Try again, then the rest or Discard all.
  • ✓✓ Saved appears sooner: the app asks every 15 seconds for the first 3 minutes after a change.
  • Solution 1.10.0.0 – quicker. The flows look for new changes every minute instead of every 2, and work on up to five at once instead of one at a time, so five quick changes take about a minute, not five. A change that finds a record still being updated by an earlier change waits its turn (re-reading every 15 seconds for up to 3 minutes) instead of being refused. Meetings, notices and issues refuse a clash with someone else plainly instead of overwriting. The queue monitor runs every 10 minutes in working hours, so a stalled run is picked up within 10 minutes.
Earlier in release 1.2 (solution 1.9.0.6 and app 1.2.4):
  • Start meeting opens the live screen at once. The agenda is fixed in the background, and outcomes you record meanwhile stay with their items.
  • Finalise shows the minutes at once, marked as being saved. The PDF follows a few minutes later.
  • The meeting screen stays still while it catches up: it no longer flashes or rebuilds when things are saved, and stays on the item you're on, even if you go through the agenda quickly.
  • 7 Minutes (finalising and amending minutes) runs every 2 minutes on weekdays 07:00–19:00, and not at all outside those hours. Its runs with nothing to do now end in seconds. Outside those hours a line under the top bar says that minutes and their PDF are completed from 07:00 on the next weekday.
  • Every flow gets to its work a little sooner: the steps it sets up first now run side by side.
  • A meeting booked a moment ago can be started straight away. What you record is sent as soon as the booking is saved. Raising or closing actions from it waits for the booking, usually under a minute.

What changes for people using the Hub

  • Changes show at once. When you raise, move, finish or change something, the screen shows it straight away, marked ✓ Sent. When it is recorded, the mark turns into a green ✓✓ Saved, which fades after a few seconds. You can carry on meanwhile; buttons don't disappear.
  • One line under the top bar says Everything is up to date, or what is still saving. It replaces the yellow strips, so the page no longer jumps.
  • If a change can't be saved, a card says why, with Try again and Dismiss, and the screen goes back to how the record really is. If someone else changed it first, nothing was changed. If the system had a fault, an administrator has been told. Trying again is safe either way: it can never make the change twice.
  • Home: Mark as done on each to-do card (then Reopen if it was a slip). There are no summaries beside the headings, and a week with nothing in it is one line.
  • Raise an action from the top bar no longer chooses an area for you (the Flask problem). Step 2 asks Which area is it for?, and the summary names the area in large type.
  • Notices and issues fit the screen: the notice on the left, comments on the right. Less-used buttons are under More for this notice or More for this issue.
  • Actions: More for this action holds Edit this action, Give it to someone else, Change its status and Close this action.
  • Meetings:
    • Items are labelled Action, Issue, Notice or Other business, and each offers only its own buttons:
      • actions: Close this action and Raise a further action;
      • issues and notices: Turn into an action.
    • + Add an item sits with the agenda, and the new item opens straight away.
    • Finalised minutes have Open the PDF, Download PDF and Amend these minutes.
    • The minutes and the PDF use people's Hub names. They list Actions closed at this meeting, and the actions raised at the meeting with their owner and due date.
  • Overview of all areas: the numbers are coloured badges; click any one to see exactly those actions. Area pages: each tab has its own buttons at the top.
  • Administration gains How long changes take: for the last 7 days, how long each kind of change took to be picked up and saved.
